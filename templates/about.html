About Smart College Predictor

Smart College Predictor is a state-of-the-art intelligent counseling decision engine designed to help students evaluate engineering college admissions with high-confidence predictive analytics across KCET and EAMCET entrance data.

Ensemble ML Engine

Utilizes calibrated Random Forest regressors and decision classification to simulate admission likelihood percentages across Safe Bets, Targets, and Reach campuses.

Verified Database

Integrates 1,300+ institutional and cutoff records spanning Karnataka and Telangana / Andhra Pradesh state counseling boards with verified seat metrics.

Multi-Factor Filtering

Filter institutions dynamically by state, engineering branch, minimum accreditation rating, cutoff cushion, and maximum management tuition fees.
