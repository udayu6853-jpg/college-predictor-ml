How Can We Help?

Learn how to use the College Predictor System.

🎓

Browse Colleges

Open the Colleges page to explore available KCET and EAMCET colleges. You can search and filter colleges by exam, branch, state, rating and management fees.

🤖

College Predictor

Select your entrance exam, enter your rank and choose your preferred branch. The system analyzes the available college data and displays matching colleges.

📊

Prediction Results

Your prediction results show matching colleges, cutoff information, ratings, fees, locations and an estimated chance indicator.

🗺️

College Location

Open any college details page to view its location. The Google Maps option searches using the college name and location.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I predict colleges?
Open the Predictor page, select KCET or EAMCET, enter your rank and select a branch. Then click Predict Colleges.
Can I view both KCET and EAMCET colleges?
Yes. The Colleges page contains records from both KCET and EAMCET datasets.
How can I see college fees?
Open the college details page. The Fee Structure section displays management fees, entrance exam fees and hostel fees when available.
How can I find the college location?
Open College Details and select Open in Google Maps. The search uses the college name together with its location and state.
Is the chance percentage a guaranteed admission probability?
No. The chance indicator is an estimate generated from the available project data and prediction logic. It should not be treated as a guaranteed admission probability.
