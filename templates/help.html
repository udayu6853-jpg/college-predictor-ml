Help & Platform Guidance

How Can We Help You?

Everything you need to know about predicting college admissions, evaluating cutoff cushions, and navigating state counseling databases.

Browsing & Filtering Colleges

Access the Colleges directory to explore over 500 accredited institutions across Karnataka (KCET) and Andhra Pradesh / Telangana (EAMCET). Use the dynamic filter bar to narrow results by state, branch, rating, or management tuition fees.

Running Admission Predictions

Select your entrance examination, enter your official merit rank, and specify your desired engineering specialization (CSE, AIML, ECE, ISE, etc.). The machine learning engine correlates your rank against historical closing trends to calculate your admission odds.

Confidence Ratings & Cushions

Predictions are segmented into three distinct categories: Safe Bets (>75%) where your rank sits comfortably within historical cutoffs, Target (50-75%), and Reach (<50%) for ambitious options.

Multi-College Comparison Matrix

Check the "Compare" box on up to three colleges in the directory, then click Compare Selected Colleges to launch a side-by-side evaluation matrix detailing cutoff ranks, tuition rates, and accreditation ratings.
